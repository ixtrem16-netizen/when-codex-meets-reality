# Building and validating a 90-minute technical training deck

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: validated draft

This milestone records the assembly of a French-language technical training deck from a structured content inventory and a reusable visual system.

## Scope

- 64 slides mapped to a 90-minute facilitation plan
- 64 speaker-note blocks
- a source block in every slide's notes
- reusable SVG and PNG visual assets
- four explicit placeholders reserved for authorized real-world evidence

## Validation loop

The presentation was exported to PowerPoint, then imported again and rendered from the exported file. The round-trip inspection confirmed:

- 64 of 64 slides present
- 64 of 64 speaker-note blocks present
- 64 of 64 source blocks present
- 64 of 64 slides rendered successfully
- full-frame geometry preserved at 1280 × 720
- no structural overflow or overlap warnings

Representative rendered slides and all source visuals were also inspected visually. Several diagrams were revised when their connectors implied a sequence or dependency that the teaching point did not support.

## Evidence discipline

The four evidence placeholders remain visible by design. The draft is therefore marked `PASS_DRAFT_WITH_4_PLACEHOLDERS`; it will become a final presentation only after authorized captures or data replace them.

The complete deck stays in private project storage. This public note records the method and validation result without publishing client or operational material.
